================================

Z-Anatomy, commit 04d15340bad66394659c0e3b3ff9d9bb0924f6ca:

Export procedure: scripts/export_z_anatomy.py using Blender 4.3.2 in
--disable-autoexec mode. CURVE/SURFACE objects are evaluated and converted to
full-detail meshes because GLB cannot represent Blender curves. FONT labels,
lights, and cameras are deliberately excluded as non-anatomical scene UI.

Browser adaptations and exclusions:
- scripts/adapt_materials.py preserves source material assignments while
  adapting GLB material properties for browser rendering; it does not alter
  mesh topology or decimate geometry. Material provenance is recorded in
  public/data/material-provenance.json.
- The cardiovascular payload is split losslessly into -01.glb and -02.glb by
  scripts/split_glb.py for the static-host asset-size limit. The original GLB
  is retained under .source/.
- Ciliary body-curve.l and Ciliary body-curve.r are excluded construction
  curves: each has bevel depth 0 and no bevel object, so neither renders as
  anatomical tissue. Their empty GLB-node anatomy extras are removed by
  scripts/finalize_asset_metadata.py.
- Vertex-only `.i` landmarks are metadata annotations, not renderable model
  surfaces. finalize_asset_metadata.py derives their positions from source
  HOOK endpoints and records provenance plus source-bounds validation.

Final metadata command (after exporting, splitting, and material adaptation):
/private/tmp/blender-app/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background
--factory-startup --disable-autoexec .source/Startup.blend --python
scripts/finalize_asset_metadata.py -- public/data/anatomy.json
